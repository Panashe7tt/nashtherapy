Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about privacy, booking, sessions and student support.

Is my information private and confidential?

Yes. All communication is end-to-end encrypted. We never share your details with third parties. You can delete your session data at any time from your profile settings.

How do I book a therapy session?

Create an account, browse therapists, filter by specialisation or gender, view availability, choose a date and time, and confirm. The whole process takes only a few minutes.

Can I choose the gender of my therapist?

Yes. Many students prefer a therapist of a specific gender. Use the gender filter on the Therapists page to find someone you feel comfortable with.

What types of sessions are available?

You can choose video calls or secure text messaging. Both options are private and can be mixed according to your preference and schedule.

I have limited data. Can I still use the site?

Yes. Key self-help resources and exercises work as a progressive web app and remain available even with low or no data connection.

Are the therapists qualified?

All therapists are licensed professionals with experience supporting university students. Profiles show credentials, years of experience, specialisations and student ratings.

How do I cancel or reschedule a session?

Go to your Dashboard, open the upcoming session, and choose Reschedule or Cancel. You will receive a confirmation message.

Is Nash Therapy a replacement for emergency help?

No. If you are in crisis or need immediate help, please contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline. Nash Therapy is for ongoing, non-emergency support.
